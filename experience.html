Working principles

What stays consistent.

01

Clarity over decoration

If an element does not help someone understand or act, it has to earn its place.

02

Accessibility is a material

Contrast, keyboard access, and clear language are part of the design, not a final checklist.

03

Ship the simplest useful thing

A smaller, well-made release teaches you more than a large speculative one.

04

Document the decisions

Good documentation turns a one-off design into something a team can carry forward.
