About me

Engineering high-throughput data platforms and production ML.

I'm Ashley Miller, a Data Science Engineer specializing in distributed streaming architectures, sub-millisecond feature stores, and calibrated machine learning models designed for high-concurrency production systems.

The technical profile

Bridging statistical theory and distributed production infrastructure.

My work sits at the intersection of applied machine learning and distributed systems engineering. That means formulating statistical loss functions, designing reproducible PySpark and Kafka pipelines, eliminating training-serving skew, and deploying low-latency Triton or Ray Serve inference clusters.

I partner with data teams, applied research groups, and engineering leaders who need senior hands-on architectural leadership across the full data lifecycle—from raw streaming events to calibrated model predictions.

How I work

Core principles behind the systems.

Operational habits that keep production pipelines resilient and models calibrated.

Statistical Rigor

Principled hypothesis testing, calibration curves, and drift detection to prevent silent degradation in production.

Zero Feature Skew

Latency is an SLA Feature

Reproducible MLOps
