About me

A designer who likes the build as much as the idea.

I'm Ashley Miller, an independent designer and front-end developer. I care about the quiet decisions that make a product easier to understand: clear structure, honest language, accessible interaction, and a visual system that holds together.

The short version

Design and development, in the same conversation.

My work sits between product design and front-end engineering. That means I can move from a fuzzy brief to a content model, from wireframes to a responsive interface, and from a design file to semantic HTML and CSS without losing the thread.

I'm most useful to small teams that need a senior pair of hands across the whole arc of a project. I ask a lot of questions early, make the constraints visible, and try to leave behind something a team can maintain after the launch.

How I work

A few principles behind the work.

Not a manifesto, just the habits that keep projects honest and useful.

Curiosity before assumptions

Understand the people, the content, and the constraints before choosing a direction.

Craft in the details

Focus states, empty states, and error messages are part of the design, not an afterthought.

Collaboration without theatre

Short feedback loops, visible decisions, and plain language make teams faster.

Systems over shortcuts

A small set of shared foundations beats a folder of one-off screens every time.
