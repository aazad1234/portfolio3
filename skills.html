How I work

A workflow built around decisions.

The tools change; the rhythm does not. Understand the problem, frame the scope, explore, refine, and ship with documentation.

01

Understand

Talk to the people who use the thing, read the content, and map the constraints.

02

Frame

Write the brief, define the information architecture, and choose the smallest useful scope.

03

Explore

Sketch broadly, prototype early, and test the interaction model before polishing pixels.

04

Refine

Build the system, check the edge cases, and make accessibility part of the definition of done.

05

Ship

Launch with documentation, a performance check, and a clear plan for what happens next.
