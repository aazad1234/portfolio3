The brief

Sleep apps often borrow the language of fitness: streaks, scores, and targets. That framing can be useful for training, but it is a poor fit for rest. Lumen explores a softer model where the product notices patterns and offers a small next step instead of grading the night.

The concept covers two related moments: winding down at the end of the day and finding focus during it. Both needed to feel low-pressure, quick to use, and respectful of attention.

Research direction

I reviewed public research on sleep hygiene and digital wind-down routines, then translated the recurring themes into product principles: reduce decisions, avoid bright stimulation, and make the default path short. These principles informed the tone, the colour system, and the number of controls on each screen.

The interface uses a dark, low-contrast palette for evening sessions and a warmer neutral palette for daytime focus. Motion is restrained and always optional. Nothing autoplays, and every soundscape has a visible stop control.

The interaction model

The home screen is a single card with two choices: wind down or focus. Choosing one opens a short session setup with a duration, a sound, and an optional reminder. The controls are sized for one hand, with the primary action anchored near the bottom of the screen.

A pattern view shows bedtime consistency and focus sessions as a soft timeline rather than a score. Copy is written in plain language, and the app explains what it does and does not infer from the data. Users can delete individual entries or turn off pattern insights entirely.

Prototype notes

The prototype covers onboarding, session setup, an active session, and the pattern view. It also includes empty, loading, and permission-denied states, which are often the first thing a real user encounters and the last thing a design file accounts for.

A production version would need clinical review of any health-related claims, real audio content, and a privacy model that is clear enough to understand without a legal background.
