The brief

The studio wanted to sit between two worlds: the precision of horticulture and the ease of a neighbourhood garden shop. The identity needed to feel knowledgeable without becoming clinical, and personal without becoming rustic.

The brand would appear on plant labels, workshop cards, packaging, and a small website. Each application had different constraints, so the system had to be flexible from the start.

Building the identity

The wordmark uses a high-contrast serif with a slightly open aperture, paired with a monogram that reads as both a leaf and a growing mark. A secondary sans-serif handles labels, schedules, and digital interfaces.

The palette pairs a deep forest green with a chalky sage, a warm paper tone, and a single clay accent. Photography and illustration are treated as equal citizens, which gives the studio room to use found imagery or commissioned botanical drawings.

Voice and applications

The voice is knowledgeable, direct, and a little wry. Plant care instructions avoid mystique and focus on the conditions a plant actually needs. Workshop descriptions say what participants will make and take home.

Applications include a label system, seed packet sleeves, workshop cards, a tote, and a responsive one-page site. The web layout uses the same type scale and colour tokens as the print system, so the brand feels consistent without looking copied and pasted.
