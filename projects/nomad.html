Hierarchical Time Series Forecasting

Forecasting SKU-level demand requires reconciling aggregate national forecasts with volatile local hub fluctuations. Nomad implements a hierarchical reconciliation framework (MinT) that guarantees mathematical consistency across all regional levels.

Models produce probabilistic quantile forecasts (p10, p50, p90), enabling inventory planners to make risk-weighted procurement decisions.

Scalable Spark Execution

Running daily forecasts across 15 million time-series combinations required fine-grained partition management in PySpark. We optimized Delta Lake z-ordering on SKU and location keys, reducing read I/O by 78%.

The batch pipeline completes in 35 minutes, providing refreshed forecasts before morning logistics shifts commence.
