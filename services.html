A simple process

Short feedback loops, fewer surprises.

The exact steps change with the project, but the rhythm stays the same: understand, define, design, build, and test.

  1. 01

    Discover & Profile

    We audit the data landscape, inspect schema definitions, and profile system constraints. I align technical objectives with measurable business criteria.

  2. 02

    Architect & Formalize

    We establish strict schema contracts, loss surfaces, and latency targets. Clear benchmarks are agreed upon before building pipelines.

  3. 03

    Engineer & Validate

    Pipelines and models are built iteratively with automated unit tests, cross-validation, and integration benchmarks under simulated production load.

  4. 04

    Optimize & Harden

    We apply kernel optimizations, weight quantization, connection pooling, and stress-test failover mechanisms.

  5. 05

    Deploy & Telemetry

    We release via canary or shadow mode with live drift monitoring, latency dashboards, and full operational runbooks.

Good to know

Questions I hear before a project starts.

What does a typical engagement look like?

Engagements typically range from six to twelve weeks for platform architecture or model deployment. I integrate closely with your data engineering or platform team, participating in sprints and conducting technical reviews.

How do you prevent training-serving skew?

I enforce shared feature definitions between offline training jobs and online streaming pipelines using declarative feature stores (e.g., Feast), immutable artifact registries, and strict schema contracts.

Can you work with our existing cloud stack?

Yes. I have extensive experience across AWS (EMR, Kinesis, S3, SageMaker), GCP (BigQuery, Vertex, Dataflow), and on-premise Kubernetes clusters running Spark, Kafka, and Triton.

Do you provide code, infrastructure, or advisory?

All three. Engagements yield production-ready, thoroughly tested Python/SQL/C++ code, declarative Terraform/Helm infrastructure recipes, and comprehensive architectural documentation.

How do you evaluate model performance beyond offline metrics?

Offline AUC/F1 is only the first checkpoint. I design comprehensive evaluation suites including calibration plots, slicing analysis across subgroups, stress tests with synthetic outliers, and online A/B experimentation frameworks.
