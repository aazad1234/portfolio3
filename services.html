A simple process

Short feedback loops, fewer surprises.

The exact steps change with the project, but the rhythm stays the same: understand, define, design, build, and test.

  1. 01

    Discover

    We start with the problem, the audience, and the constraints. I ask enough questions to avoid designing the wrong thing beautifully.

  2. 02

    Define

    Research becomes a clear brief, a content model, and a set of priorities. We agree on what success looks like before visual work begins.

  3. 03

    Design

    I work from structure to surface: flows, wireframes, then high-fidelity interfaces. Feedback happens in short, focused rounds.

  4. 04

    Build

    Design and implementation move together. I build responsive, accessible front ends or work closely with your engineering team.

  5. 05

    Test & launch

    We check the details that matter: keyboard access, performance, edge cases, and real content. Then we ship and document what comes next.

Good to know

Questions I hear before a project starts.

How long does a typical project take?

Most focused projects run between four and twelve weeks. A single-page site is usually faster than a multi-flow product. After a short discovery call I can give you a realistic scope and timeline rather than a guess.

Do you work with existing teams?

Yes. I can join a product team for a defined piece of work, collaborate with in-house designers, or hand off a system to engineers. Clear communication and documented decisions are part of the engagement.

What does a project cost?

Every engagement is scoped around the work, not a generic package. After we agree on the outcomes, I provide a fixed project fee or a monthly retainer. I do not publish prices because a useful estimate depends on scope.

Can you build what you design?

For websites and front-end work, yes. I build semantic, responsive interfaces and can work within your stack. For complex back-end or native mobile work, I collaborate with your engineers or a trusted development partner.

What happens after launch?

You receive the source files, documentation, and a short handoff session. I also offer a limited post-launch support window for fixes and small refinements. Ongoing retainers are available for teams that need regular design support.
